What Fora does

The long way is the point.

Fora pairs the drives worth taking slowly with the places worth stopping for — curated, editorial, and quietly in your pocket.

Scenic drives

Curated routes worth the long way around — coastal cliffs, forest switchbacks, backroads — with the stops that make them.

Third places

The spots between home and work: cafés, bookshops, parks, bars, and diners worth settling into for an afternoon.

Drives & places, together

Every drive threads through third places, so the route is also an invitation to pull over and stay a while.

Save your routes

Keep collections of drives and places. Saved on your device — never uploaded to a server.

Add a place or a drive

Know a road or a spot others should find? Submit it for editorial review before it joins the shared catalog.

Private by design

No account to create, no ads, no third-party tracking. Your location is used on-device to find what's nearby.

Every kind of long way

Pick a mood, not a destination.

Drives are grouped by the kind of landscape you're after — so you can chase a coastline on Saturday and a forest switchback next week.

Coastal

Cliff roads and sea-level causeways with the water always in frame.

Mountain

Switchbacks, passes, and overlooks that earn the altitude.

Backroads

Two-lane detours through small towns and nobody's hurry.

Forest

Canopy roads and redwood groves under a green ceiling.

Lakeside

Shoreline loops and reflections that make you slow down.

Desert

Open horizons, big light, and roads that run to the edge of it.

The quiet part

Built to respect you.

Fora collects as little as possible. It works the moment you open it — no sign-up, no profile, no sales pitch.

No account

Opens anonymously. We never ask for your name or email.

No tracking

No third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. No ad identifiers.

On-device location

Used to show what's nearby. It isn't transmitted to or stored by us.

Your saves, yours

Saved drives and places stay on your device and leave when you delete the app.
